My dearest Avline,

If I could live my life again and undo every mistake, I wouldn’t.

Every wrong turn I ever took was part of the path that led to you — and the odds of us meeting were too small to gamble with twice.

A life full of mistakes with you is better than a flawless one without you.

The truth is, I have never been very good at telling you what you actually mean to me.

So let me try, just this once — slowly, and in my own hand.

You have a way of making the ordinary feel like something worth remembering.

And on a day that is entirely yours, I wanted to give you the one thing a screen could never quite hold.

Happy birthday.

— Ari

some things were never meant for a screen.
